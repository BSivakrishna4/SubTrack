## 1. Survey Objective
The objective of this survey is to identify recurring pain points faced by consumers and SaaS managers regarding subscription billing, automated renewals, cancellation difficulty, and status tracking visibility.

---

## 2. Target Respondents
- Active digital service subscribers (Streaming, SaaS tools, Gyms, News).
- Small business owners and platform administrators managing subscription billing.

## 5. Common Issues Identified (From Survey Template Focus)

1. **Unexpected Auto-Renewals**: Users are charged without clear pre-expiry notification.
2. **Hidden Cancellation Options**: Cancelling a plan often requires navigating complex support tickets.
3. **Lack of Centralized Status Tracking**: Users lose track of start and expiry dates across multiple services.
4. **Payment Failure Friction**: When payment fails, accounts are abruptly locked without clear retry mechanisms.
